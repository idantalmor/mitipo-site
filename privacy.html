Mitipo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Mitipo is a private household assistant that runs in one family's WhatsApp group. It is not offered to the public.

Information Mitipo handles

  • Messages sent in the WhatsApp group it is connected to.
  • Receipts, documents and screenshots sent to it as images or PDFs.
  • Information it records from them, such as expenses, account balances, reminders, notes, shopping lists and maintenance logs.

How it is used

This information is used only to provide Mitipo's features to the members of the group: answering them, saving what they ask it to save, and sending reminders and summaries.

Where it is stored

Mitipo stores its data on the computer it runs on.

Google Drive

Mitipo connects to the Google Drive of its own dedicated Google account, to store and retrieve files such as receipts and documents and to create the folders that hold them. Its Google access token is stored only on the computer it runs on. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for these purposes.

Sharing

Mitipo uses the Anthropic Claude API to understand messages and attachments and to write its replies, so that content is sent to Anthropic for processing. Mitipo's messages travel through WhatsApp, and files saved to Drive are held by Google. Information is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Removing data or access

To have information deleted, contact the address below. Access to a Google account can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
