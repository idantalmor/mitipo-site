Mitipo

A private household assistant for WhatsApp.

Mitipo is a personal assistant that runs in one family's private WhatsApp group. It records expenses, reads and keeps receipts and documents, manages reminders and shopping lists, and answers questions about what it has saved.

Mitipo uses Google Drive to store and retrieve its own files in the Drive of its dedicated Google account.

Mitipo is a private project. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
